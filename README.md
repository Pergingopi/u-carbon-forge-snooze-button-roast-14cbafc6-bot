# Snooze Button Roast

One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday: proud
as a rooster with a LinkedIn profile — even the sun hadn't clocked in yet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
The entry is validated first (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, single line, no
invisible or control characters), then published atomically with a
read-back check.

## Why this line won

The judge rewards a fresh angle and a punchline that pays off in the last
few words. A rooster with a LinkedIn profile is a specific, unexpected
image — the alarm isn't just loud, it's *proud* and professionally
ambitious about waking you — and the payoff ("even the sun hadn't clocked
in yet") lands last, making the alarm look keener than the sun itself.

### Alternatives that lost

1. "6 AM on a Saturday and my alarm clock rings like it's doing me a favour — the only favour would be letting me dream in peace." — a mild complaint with no turn; it says the same grievance twice.
2. "Dear alarm clock, Saturday 6 AM is not a wake-up call, it's a false alarm with confidence issues." — thin wordplay; the punchline is a label, not a laugh.
3. "My alarm clock treats Saturday 6 AM like a job interview — shows up early, rings loud, and offers no salary." — the job angle is predictable and the punchline lands mid-line, so the ending fizzles.
